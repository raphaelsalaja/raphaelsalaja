Animations and transitions do more than just add flair — they shape the tone of your interface. Used thoughtfully, they can make a design feel playful and energetic, or calm and professional.

Think of tone as a spectrum. You can adjust where your product sits on that spectrum by tweaking animation speed, easing, and style. A fast, bouncy motion might feel lighthearted and fun, while slower, smoother transitions tend to feel more refined and serious.
