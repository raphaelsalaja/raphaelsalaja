Each motion curve carries its own character. Springs bounce with energy, eases flow with grace, speed dictates urgency. Picking the right one means matching mood to movement.

# Design with Intention

Springs bring life and playfulness - great for friendly notifications or buttons that jiggle on hover. Eases feel natural and comforting - perfect for cards sliding in or images fading gently.

Speed and bounce define your tone. Fast springs feel snappy and confident, while slow eases invite reflection. Mix quick entrances with gentle exits to give each interaction its own voice. Test in context - tweak stiffness, damping and duration until your motions delight and guide users with clarity.
