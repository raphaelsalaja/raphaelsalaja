The white T-shirt is a paradox. It is the most basic garment in menswear, yet it quietly defines personal style. In an era fixated on novelty, the white tee endures because it's the ultimate exercise in restraint, when done well, it disappears into your life and becomes indispensable.

# What Separates Good from Great

Not all white T-shirts are created equal. The difference between a disposable tee and a wardrobe staple comes down to obsessive attention to quality.

High quality brands invest years of research into refining fabric and construction, resulting in dense, tactile jerseys and seamless finishes.

You should be looking for bound collars with reinforced stitching resist stretching over time, while tubular or loopwheeled construction avoids side seams and increases durability.

Look for mid-to-heavyweight cotton (typically 7-9 oz / 230-260 gsm) that provides structure and longevity, paired with a cut that fits your shape.

# Recommendations

With so many options available, these three are ones I'd recommend.

## Merz b. Schwanen 215 Loopwheeled Classic Fit - $100+

The Merz b. Schwanen 215 Loopwheeled Classic Fit is a testament to the brand's commitment to quality and craftsmanship. It's probably the most sought-after white tee on the market.

It gained a cult following among fashion enthusiasts post it's inclusion in The Bear TV Series. Carmy (Jeremy Allen White) wears it throughout the series, showcasing its versatility and timeless appeal.

Be mindful however, within the show, Jeremy's cut has been tailored, giving it a more fitted silhouette. When you receive it, you may find that the fit is more relaxed.

![Merz b. Schwanen 215 Loopwheeled Classic Fit](/library/white-tees/merz-b-schwanen-215.jpg)

Each piece is handmade in Germany, so you’ll notice subtle variations which is also part of its artisanal charm.

It's rather expensive, but many consider it worth the investment.

## Whitesville Tubular T-Shirt - $90

The Whitesville Tubular T-Shirt White stands out as a modern classic that pays meticulous attention to both form and function.

It also gained popularity after being featured heavily in The Bear.

Outside of this, it's a wonderfully crafted tee. Crafted in Japan from 100% long-staple cotton, it delivers a seamless tubular body and a fit inspired by vintage American tees.

![Whitesville Tubular T-Shirt Packaging](/library/white-tees/whitesville-2.jpg)

Another bonus is that it comes in a two-pack. However due to its popularity, it can be hard to find in stock, so be sure to grab it if you see it available.

## Bronson MFG Loopwheel Tubular Athletic T-Shirt - $30

The Bronson MFG Loopwheel Tubular Athletic T-Shirt is a standout option for those seeking a blend of comfort and style. Made in Japan, it features a unique loopwheel construction that results in a soft, durable fabric with a vintage feel.

![Bronson MFG Loopwheel Tubular Athletic T-Shirt](https://bronsonshop.com/cdn/shop/products/4_98128c3f-6eba-4492-913d-b33e849b36d4.jpg?v=1723604087)

The main benefit of the Bronson is how cheap it is. It's the cheapest out of the three, coming in at almost 1/3 the price of the Merz b. Schwanen. I'd recommend it if you're looking for a more affordable option that still maintains quality.
