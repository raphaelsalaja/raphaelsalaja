---
title: "Nature"
description: "Transition is a process, not a switch."
---

Nothing in nature instantly becomes something else. It eases, unfolds, and transforms slowly through phase, motion, and time. From a cloud forming to a leaf turning, change is a process, not a switch.

<Stage>

</Stage>

Your UI should do the same. Transitions shouldn’t snap, They should flow. Give them room to breathe, to guide, to feel alive. Let motion carry meaning, not just movement.
