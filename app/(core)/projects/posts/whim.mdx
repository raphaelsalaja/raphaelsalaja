Whim transcends the traditional digital library, merging aesthetic elegance with robust
functionality. Users can curate and explore collections of unique and iconic items within a
seamless, visually stunning interface.

<Image src='/projects/whim/image-2.png' alt='whim home page' caption='Whim ― Home' />

Whim addresses a market need for a sophisticated, user-friendly platform tailored to creatives. Its
versatile search capabilities by designer, time period, color palette, and more make it an
invaluable tool for those in pursuit of the perfect piece.

<Image src='/projects/whim/image-1.png' alt='whim detail page' caption='Whim ― Detail' />

Designed to be the ultimate search engine for visual inspiration, Whim empowers users to discover,
collect, and share their favorite items effortlessly. Whether you're a designer, artist, or an
admirer of beautiful things, Whim provides a curated experience that is both inspiring and
practical.

Whim is expected to launch in early 2025.
